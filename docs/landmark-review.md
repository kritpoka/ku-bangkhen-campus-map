## Dropped / not verified

- **พระพิรุณทรงนาค** (official KU landmark): OSM has two conflicting nodes ~700 m apart (n1374641626 and n1374746784). KU says it's "in front of อาคารสุวรรณวาจกกสิกิจ", which isn't mapped in OSM.
- **หออนุสรณ์ 60 ปี / Time Capsule** (official KU landmarks): not mapped; only a "สวน 60 ปี" area exists (w259496906).
- **Gymnasium**: listed on ku.ac.th but no confident OSM match.
- **Hospital**: KU's Faculty of Medicine hospital is under construction; no verified on-campus location. The Infirmary is listed instead.
- **BTS Kasetsart University (N13)** and other transit: outside the boundary, so not interactive. KU shuttle stops will live in the shuttle overlay (OSM has 39 stops tagged `network=Kasetsart University`).
